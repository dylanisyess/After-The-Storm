# Final metrics

Generated by `pipeline/10_final_metrics.py` on 2026-10-24 from the saved model (trained on 95,477 beach samples, 2010-2022). Test period: 2023-01-02 to 2026-10-17, never used for training.

**How to read this.** Percentages are shares of *tested beach-days* (days a beach was sampled), not calendar days. "Unsafe" means enterococcus over 104 MPN/100 mL. Each rule is compared with the model warning on the same number of days, so the question is: with the same amount of warning, which catches more unsafe days? Ranges in parentheses are 95% ranges from resampling whole beaches 1,000 times. Retraining the model can shift results by about ±0.5 points, so don't quote more precision than that.

**Today's rules.** *Signs*: a warning is posted when the latest enterococcus result is over the limit, one day after sampling (when the lab result comes back). *Rain advisory*: LA County's advice to avoid the water for 72 hours after 0.1 inch of rain. *Three-indicator signs*: a warning when any of the three state ocean standards fails (enterococcus, fecal coliform, total coliform), which is what counties post on. The model uses results at least 2 days old, because public data takes longer to appear online.

## Quotable summary

- On 30,262 tested beach-days from January 2023 to October 2026 that the model never saw in training, it caught **66% of unsafe days**, compared with 61% for warning signs plus LA County's rain advisory, with the same number of warnings (21% of tested days). Difference: +5.0 points; 95% range +1.9 to +8.6.
- Against warning signs alone, at the same number of warnings: 53% vs 46% of unsafe days (+7.1 points; 95% range +3.7 to +12.2).
- Against signs based on all three state standards, plus the rain advisory: 68% vs 62% (+5.3 points; 95% range +2.1 to +9.1).
- Estimated live accuracy, using the rain data the live app actually gets: 66% vs 61% for signs + rain advisory (+4.7 points; 95% range +1.5 to +8.4).
- Three days ahead, assuming a good rain forecast: 63% of unsafe days caught vs 61% for the signs + rain advisory posted on the day itself (+1.4 points; 95% range -2.8 to +6.7). The range includes zero, so say "about as good as", not "better than".
- ROC-AUC 0.859 (0.840 to 0.874).

## Threshold fixed in advance

The app warns when risk ≥ 0.159, chosen to give the same number of warnings as signs + rain advisory on the test years (no test results are used to choose it). As a check, a threshold set using 2022 only (0.164) and applied unchanged to 2023 onward warns on 20.8% of tested days and catches 65.1% of unsafe days (43.7% of warnings right).

## Caveats

- Results are for beach-days that were actually sampled. After a bad result, beaches are often resampled, so unsafe days are over-represented compared with all calendar days.
- The sign baselines are reconstructed from the data, not from records of actual postings.
- Live mode (B) is an estimate from archived forecast-model rain. Real 1-3 day rain forecasts are less accurate than that, so later forecast days will do somewhat worse than B and C suggest.
